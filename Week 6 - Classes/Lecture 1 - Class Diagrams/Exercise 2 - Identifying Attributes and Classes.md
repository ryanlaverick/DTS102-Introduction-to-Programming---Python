# Task 1
Look at the following description of a system and list the nouns and verbs:

A vet, who lives in Sunderland, treats dogs and cats. The total time spent treating a dog or cat is
calculated. Dogs and cats have a guardian and an address. Bills are generated. The vet posts these bills
to the owner. The vet also treats any cat found wandering the streets - these cats do not have an
owner. The vet wants a system to keep track of the time spent treating cats and dogs and print bills.

## Nouns
- Vet
- Sunderland
- Dogs
- Cats
- Guardian
- Guardian Address
- Bills

## Verbs
- Treating
- Calculated
- Generated
- Posting

# Task 2
Look at the description and identify …
- one thing outside the scope of the system,
- a synonym
- a noun that could become a class
- a noun that would probably be an attribute
- a verb that could become a method
- classes that may have common attributes and methods

## One thing outside the scope of the system
Tracking where the Cat or Dog was found.

## A synonym
Calculated and Generated could be classed as synonyms.

## A noun that could become a class
Cat or Dog.

## A noun that would probably be an attribute
Guardian -- who is looking after the Cat or Dog while they are undergoing treatment.

## A verb that could become a method
Generated -- generating the bill for the time the Cat or Dog was treated for.

## Classes that may have common attributes and methods
Dogs & Cats.
